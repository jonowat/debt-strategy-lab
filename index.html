How to use this tool (Click to expand)
  1. Set your Budget: Enter the total monthly amount you can afford to pay towards debts.
  2. Add Debts: Create 'Card' groups and add balance segments (e.g., Purchases, Balance Transfers) with their respective APRs.
  3. Simulate Transfers: Add available "Balance Transfer Offers" to see if moving high-interest debt saves you money.
  4. Choose Strategy: Toggle between strategies (Avalanche, Snowball, etc.) to see which gets you debt-free faster.
  5. Review Plan: Check the "Granular Action Plan" for a month-by-month guide on where to send your payments.
Understanding Windfalls & Balance Transfers
Windfalls
These are one-off lump sums (like a bonus, tax refund, or inheritance) that you can inject into your debt payment plan at a specific future month. The simulation applies these entirely to the principal on top of your regular monthly budget.
Balance Transfer (BT) Offers
A Balance Transfer allows you to move debt from a high-interest card to a new card with a lower rate (often 0%) for a promotional period.
  • Cap: The credit limit of the new card.
  • Fee %: One-time cost to transfer (e.g., 3%).
  • Promo APR: The low rate you pay during the offer period.
  • Months: How long the low rate lasts before reverting to the standard rate.

The tool automatically calculates the best debts to move to maximize your interest savings.

Calculations are estimates based on the information provided and may not reflect exact bank practices regarding interest compounding, payment allocation, or fees. Important: Actual results will vary. Card issuers often calculate interest based on 'Average Daily Balance' and apply payments on specific dates which affects the interest charged.
